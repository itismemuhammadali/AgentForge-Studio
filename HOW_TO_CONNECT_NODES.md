# How to Connect Nodes - Simple Step-by-Step Guide

## The Connection Process (4 Simple Steps)

### Step 1: Click on a Node to Select It
- Click anywhere on a node
- The node will get a **blue border**
- **4 colored circles** will appear on the edges (top, right, bottom, left)

### Step 2: Click on an Edge Point (the colored circle)
- Click on one of the 4 colored circles on the node's edges
- A **dashed blue line** will appear from that edge
- The line will follow your mouse cursor
- At the top, you'll see: **"Connecting... Click an edge on another node to complete"**

### Step 3: Click on the Target Node
- Move your mouse to another node
- Click on that node to select it
- The target node will also show its 4 colored circles

### Step 4: Click on an Edge Point on the Target Node
- Click on one of the 4 colored circles on the target node
- A **solid blue arrow** will appear connecting the two nodes
- The connection is now complete and permanent!

## Visual Indicators

### When You Start a Connection:
- Green status bar appears at the top saying "Connecting..."
- Dashed blue line follows your mouse
- You can see which edge you're connecting from

### When You Hover Over an Edge Point:
- The circle gets **bigger** (grows from 30px to 36px)
- The shadow gets **darker** and **larger**
- This shows you can click it

### When Connection is Complete:
- Solid blue arrow appears between the nodes
- Status bar disappears
- Arrow stays there permanently

## Example: Connecting Two Nodes Vertically

1. **Click the first node** → Blue border appears, 4 circles show up
2. **Click the bottom circle** (↓) → Dashed line appears
3. **Click the second node below** → Its circles appear
4. **Click the top circle** (↑) → Arrow connects them!

## Example: Connecting Two Nodes Horizontally

1. **Click the first node** → Blue border appears, 4 circles show up
2. **Click the right circle** (→) → Dashed line appears
3. **Click the second node to the right** → Its circles appear
4. **Click the left circle** (←) → Arrow connects them!

## Canceling a Connection

If you started a connection and want to cancel:
- Click the **X button** in the green status bar at the top
- Or click on empty canvas to deselect

## Tips

✅ **Always click on the colored circles** - not the node itself
✅ **The circles only appear when a node is selected** - click the node first
✅ **Hover over circles to see them grow** - this confirms you can click them
✅ **Watch the status bar** - it tells you what to do next
✅ **Arrows are permanent** - they stay until you delete the node

## Troubleshooting

**Q: I clicked a node but don't see the circles**
A: The circles only appear when the node is selected (blue border). Click the node again.

**Q: I clicked a circle but nothing happened**
A: Make sure you're clicking on the colored circles on the edges, not the node itself.

**Q: The dashed line disappeared**
A: You can cancel by clicking the X in the status bar. Then try again.

**Q: I don't see the arrow after clicking**
A: Make sure you clicked on an edge circle on the target node, not the node itself.

**Q: Can I connect a node to itself?**
A: No, you must select a different node for the target.
